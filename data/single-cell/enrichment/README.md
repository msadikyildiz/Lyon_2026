## Method and provenance

The analyst's screenshot of his usual settings shows EcoCyc's E. coli K-12 substrain MG1655 database, the Enrichment analysis, Fisher Exact statistic, Benjamini–Hochberg correction and P < 0.1 cutoff. It includes pathways, transcriptional/translational regulators and Gene Ontology terms. These are author-reported usual settings, not a recovered settings history for each result table. The screenshot's SmartTable (cluster 0, 319 genes) was created on 7 May 2025; it is not one of the five attached result exports.

[EcoCyc documentation, section 7.6.1](https://ecocyc.org/PToolsWebsiteHowto.shtml) describes over-representation testing, rather than a ranked-list GSEA algorithm. Its default reference sets depend on the annotation class: genes assigned to pathways for pathway testing, and genes with GO annotations for GO testing. The exports do not record the historical tested universe or database version. The headers list result object types: GO terms and pathways, with `All-Genes` in four exports and `DNA-Segments` in two. These labels do not establish which classes were tested or the statistical background. Supplied rows, including the `All-Genes` result with an empty Matches cell, are retained unchanged.

[Release notes](https://biocyc.org/ecoli/release-notes.shtml) date EcoCyc 29.0 to 10 April 2025 and 29.1 to 22 September 2025. Version 29.0 is therefore consistent with the May 2025 workbook and SmartTable dates, but remains an inference and is not assigned as the verified analysis version. Exact recalculation requires the historical annotation snapshot and the analysis-specific reference sets. Re-exporting and checking supplied results do not rerun the enrichment tests.
